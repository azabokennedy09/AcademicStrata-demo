About Academicstrata

Academicstrata began with a simple idea: students learn better when their notes are clear, original and easy to find. We write study guides, summaries and practice questions for high school students, and we share many of them for free so no learner is left behind.

Our mission

To help every student understand their subjects and approach exams with confidence.

Who writes the guides

Our guides are written by educators and subject specialists who know the syllabus and how students really study.

What we believe

  • Clear explanations beat long notes.
  • Every guide is original and written for students.
  • Good revision material should be easy to find.
