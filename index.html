About Academicstrata

Academicstrata began with a simple idea: students learn better when their notes are clear, original and easy to find. We write study guides, summaries and practice questions for high school students, and we share many of them for free so no learner is left behind.

Frequently asked questions

Detailed answers about our guides, premium access, subjects and how to reach us.

Contact us
Are the study guides free?

Many of them are. Every guide on the site is marked either Free or Premium, so you always know which kind you are opening.

  • Free guides are open to everyone. There is nothing to pay, and you can download them straight from the Resources page. They are mostly summaries and notes for core topics.
  • Premium guides carry a gold badge. They go deeper, and they are unlocked by arrangement (see “How do I get premium access?” below).

Use the filters on the Resources page to show only free guides, only premium guides, or a single subject.

What is inside a premium guide?

Premium guides are original, in-depth material written by our team. Each one is built to take you from understanding a topic to being able to use it in an exam:

  • Plain-language summaries of each topic, so the main ideas are easy to follow.
  • Step-by-step worked examples that show how a question is approached and why each step is taken.
  • Practice questions with answers, so you can test yourself and check your work straight away.

Like every guide on the site, a premium guide is a printable PDF.

How do I get premium access?

Each premium guide costs $1, paid through PayPal. The process has three steps:

  1. Choose a guide. Find it on the Premium Guides list.
  2. Pay $1. Sign in, press Pay $1 with PayPal on the guide, and complete the payment on PayPal. Please pay with the same email as your account if you can.
  3. Get access. After we confirm your payment, we unlock the guide for your account, usually within a day. If it takes longer, contact us.

If you do not have an account yet, use Sign Up at the top of the site first, so we have an account to unlock the guide for.

Which subjects do you cover?

We cover a wide range of high school subjects:

  • Mathematics

  • Physics (General Science is available for learners with special needs)
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Entrepreneurship Education
  • Kiswahili

  • English Language

  • Religious Education (CRE or IRE)

More material is added regularly, so check the Resources page for the latest uploads. If a subject or topic you need is missing, tell us on the Contact page.

Do you have A Level and O Level material?

Yes. A Level and O Level are both part of our services, and you can see them on the Services page. To find material, open the Resources page and search by subject or keyword, for example “Chemistry”. If you cannot find what you need for your level, contact us with the subject, the level and the topic, and we will let you know what is available.

Can I download and print the guides?

Yes. Every guide is a PDF, so you can:

  • save it to your phone, tablet or computer and read it later without being online;
  • print the whole guide, or only the pages you need, to write on and revise from.

Premium guides can be downloaded after they have been unlocked for your account.

Who writes the guides?

All of our content is original and is written by our team, who know the syllabus and how students really study. Every file is also reviewed by an admin before it appears on the site, and we update guides when students and teachers send us feedback. You can meet the people behind the guides on the Our Team page.

Do you offer exam preparation and tutoring?

Yes, both are listed on the Services page:

  • Exam Prep Packs are topic bundles for end-of-term and final exams.
  • Tutoring Support is available one-to-one or for a group.

Prices are given on request. Send us an enquiry on the Contact page with your subject, your level and what you want help with.

Can I share my own notes or lesson plans?

Yes. If you are a teacher or student with notes, schemes of work or lesson plans to share, you can send them to us for review:

  • Sign in and use the upload form. Files can be PDF or Word (.docx) and up to 20 MB each.
  • Uploaded files are not published straight away. An admin reviews each one and approves it before it appears on the site.
  • You can also just get in touch through the Contact page and we will tell you how to send them.
